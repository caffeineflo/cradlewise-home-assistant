# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Focus criticism on the work, give people
room to correct mistakes, and assume good intent until the evidence says
otherwise. Different levels of Home Assistant, networking, and reverse
engineering experience are welcome here.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content,
threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not pressure
anyone to disclose private account, household, device, or family information.

## Privacy and safety

Never post baby images or video, credentials, tokens, device certificates, or
private hostnames. Redact sensitive details from screenshots, logs, packet
captures, and examples before sharing them. Report authentication bypasses,
credential exposure, certificate exposure, and private media access through a
private GitHub security advisory instead of a public issue.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports in confidence and may edit or
remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions will consider the
impact and pattern of behavior, not only the stated intent.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and project-related communication elsewhere.
